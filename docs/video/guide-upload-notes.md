## The claim that must not change

The title, the description and the narration all say a tampered IMEI is
**caught** — `پکڑا جاتا ہے` — never that changing one is impossible. No fleet of
real handsets has been tested yet, and a shopkeeper who believes a phone can
never be freed will lend more against it than he should. Keep that wording if
any of this is rewritten.
