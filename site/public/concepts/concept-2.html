How it works

From sale to recovery, one flow.

Your staff need no technical skill — just a QR scan.

1 · QR enrolment

The new phone scans a QR during setup and the device-owner app installs. The customer signs the agreement on screen.

2 · Plans & payments

The plan is scheduled automatically. Record payments, print receipts, and reminders go out from your SIM.

3 · Auto lock / unlock

Past the due date the phone locks and shows the balance; once paid, it unlocks by itself.
