How it works

Three steps at the counter.

Record the sale, let the phone scan a QR, and the system keeps the book from there.

01

Enrol with a QR

Scan the QR on the new phone's setup screen. The phone comes under your shop's management, and the customer signs the agreement on screen.

02

Instalments & payments

The schedule builds itself. Every payment goes in the ledger, a receipt prints, and reminders go out from your own SIM.

03

Automatic lock & unlock

If an instalment runs late, the phone locks and shows what is owed. Record the payment and it unlocks.
