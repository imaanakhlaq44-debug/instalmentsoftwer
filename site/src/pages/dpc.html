What the app can do

  • Enrol from a QR during setup, as device owner
  • Check in on a schedule, so a queued command can reach it
  • Show a kiosk lock screen carrying the payment details
  • Restore the lock state after a reboot
  • It does not read messages, photos or calls
  • It does not block emergency calls, even while locked
  • It does not report a lock it did not actually apply
  • It cannot lock a phone with no signed agreement — the server refuses

How a phone is enrolled

  1. 1

    The phone must be new or factory reset. Device owner can only be granted during setup.

  2. 2

    Tap the welcome screen six times — Android opens its QR scanner.

  3. 3

    Scan the QR generated in the dashboard. The phone downloads the app, verifies it against the checksum, and installs it as device owner.

  4. 4

    The app takes credentials of its own and the handset comes under management. The QR works exactly once.

A handset that already has the app installed can redeem the code from inside the app instead, which needs neither the file nor the checksum.

The shop's own counter phone

A second, much smaller app runs on a phone of your own. It takes the queue of reminders and sends them from that handset's SIM. It has nothing to do with the customer's phone.
