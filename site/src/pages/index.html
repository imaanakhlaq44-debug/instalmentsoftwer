FAQ

Common questions

What if the customer turns off the internet?

The phone keeps the schedule on the device. Once the date passes, it locks even without data.

Can the customer escape with a factory reset?

Reset from Settings is blocked. And while money is owed, even a recovery-mode wipe still asks for your Google account.

Where does the payment money go?

Straight to you. We are not a payment gateway — you record the payment and the phone opens.

What if I run out of locks?

A new financed sale won't register — you find out at the counter, not later. Buy a pack and the same QR completes.

Qiston par mobile bechne ka software kya karta hai?

Har customer ka advance, mahana qist aur baqaya ek digital khatay mein rehta hai. Yaad-dehani ka SMS aap ki apni SIM se jata hai, aur qist ki tareekh guzar jaye to phone khud lock ho jata hai.

Qist ada hone par mobile kaise khulta hai?

Aap dashboard par payment darj karte hain aur phone agli baar net milte hi khud khul jata hai. Customer ko dukaan aane ki zaroorat nahin.

Kya koi mahana fees hai?

Nahin. Aap sirf lock khareedte hain — Rs 600 se Rs 800 per phone, pack ke hisaab se. Ek phone ka lock dobara charge nahin hota.
