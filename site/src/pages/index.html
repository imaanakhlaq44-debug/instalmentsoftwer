How it works

Three simple steps at your counter.

Provisioning a phone and handing it to a customer takes under two minutes.

  1. 01

    Register the sale

    Select the handset model, advance payment, and monthly plan. The bilingual agreement drafts itself automatically.

  2. 02

    The customer signs on screen

    The buyer reads the Urdu terms and signs with their finger on your tablet or phone. Without this signature, no lock can ever be activated.

  3. 03

    The phone scans a QR

    Tap the setup screen 6 times to scan the QR. The phone enters Device Owner mode and is permanently protected against hard factory-resets.

Legal Consent

No handset is locked without explicit signed consent.

Every financed sale generates a legally binding agreement detailing the handset model, IMEI, total price, installment schedule, and explicit terms authorizing device restriction in case of genuine default.

Once signed by the customer on-screen, the contract is cryptographically frozen. Any alteration invalidates old permissions and requires fresh customer sign-off.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is this legal under Pakistani law?

Yes. A lock is strictly executed under an explicit, signed agreement between dealer and buyer authorizing device restriction upon default. Without that customer signature, the software prohibits lock activation.

What if the buyer hard factory-resets the handset?

The app is provisioned as Android Device Owner (Knox equivalent) during initial setup. A factory wipe retains management and automatically re-locks upon reboot.

Is a locked phone bricked?

No. Emergency calls (1122, 15) remain 100% active. The screen clearly guides the customer on how to pay at your shop or via JazzCash/EasyPaisa to restore full access.

Do you collect our money?

Never. All installment payments go directly to your shop counter or your own JazzCash/bank accounts. We charge only for software lock credits.
