Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is this legal under Pakistani law?

Yes. A lock is strictly executed under an explicit, signed agreement between dealer and buyer authorizing device restriction upon default. Without that customer signature, the software prohibits lock activation.

What if the buyer hard factory-resets the handset?

The app is provisioned as Android Device Owner mode during initial setup. A factory wipe retains management and automatically re-locks upon reboot.

Do installment payments go through you?

Never. All installment payments go directly to your shop counter or your own JazzCash/bank accounts. We charge only for software lock credits.

Can a locked phone make emergency calls?

Emergency calls (1122, 15) remain 100% active. The screen clearly guides the customer on how to pay at your shop or via JazzCash/EasyPaisa to restore full access.
