In simple words

Selling phones on instalments? No more chasing payments.

Almas SDM is a digital khata and a phone lock for mobile shops. This page explains how it works, without the technical words.

Does this sound familiar?

  • ❌ The customer simply stops paying.
  • ❌ They switch the phone off and disappear.
  • ❌ Every month you are calling customers to ask for money.
  • ❌ The notebook and register never quite add up.

Almas SDM solves all of this. It works in three steps.

  1. 01

    Scan the QR

    Open the new phone and scan the QR code. The customer signs the agreement on the screen with a finger. The agreement is in both Urdu and English, so the customer understands every word.

  2. 02

    Take payment, give a receipt

    Every customer's khata builds itself: advance, monthly instalment and balance, all on one screen. Before each due date, a reminder SMS goes to the customer from your own SIM.

  3. 03

    Lock and unlock, all automatic

    ⏰ If an instalment is late, the phone locks itself.

    ✅ When you record the payment, the phone opens itself. The customer does not need to come back to the shop.

🔒 The customer can't slip away

  • Turning off the internet does not stop the lock.
  • Factory reset from Settings is blocked.
  • Even after a wipe, the phone asks for your Google account.

🤝 Fair to your customer

  • The lock screen clearly shows the amount owed, the shop's name and its number.
  • Emergency calls to 1122 and 15 work even when locked.
  • We never track the customer's location.
  • The customer's photos and data are never erased.
  • No signed agreement, no lock.
