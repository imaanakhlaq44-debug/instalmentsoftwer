How it works

Just 3 steps

Your staff need no technical skill. One QR scan, and the system takes it from there.

01

Scan the QR

Open the new phone and scan the QR code. The customer signs the agreement on the screen with a finger. It is in both Urdu and English, so the customer understands every word.

02

Take payment, give a receipt

Every customer's khata builds itself: advance, monthly instalment and balance, all on one screen.

Before each due date, a reminder SMS goes to the customer from your own SIM.

03

Lock & unlock, all automatic

⏰ If an instalment is late, the phone locks itself.

✅ When you record the payment, the phone opens itself. The customer doesn't need to come back to the shop.
